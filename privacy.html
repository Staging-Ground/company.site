Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Staging Ground is a trade name of Ubuntu Equity Group, LLC, a California limited liability company ("Staging Ground," "we," "us"). This policy explains what information we collect through staginggroundlabs.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through the contact form:

  • Contact details: your name and email address.
  • Work details (optional): your company and role.
  • Your message: what you're looking for and anything you tell us about your team.
  • Your email preference: whether you asked to receive new videos and updates.

Like most websites, our hosting and font providers automatically receive basic technical information when you load a page, such as your IP address and browser type. We do not use analytics, advertising cookies, or tracking pixels on this site.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and discuss training or services you asked about.
  • To send you one welcome email after you contact us.
  • If, and only if, you tick the "Send me new videos and updates" box: to send occasional emails about new videos, resources, and events.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Who helps us handle it

We use a small number of service providers to run this site and our email:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts on this site.
  • Web3Forms delivers contact form submissions to our inbox.
  • Kit stores contacts and sends our welcome and update emails.
  • Zoho Mail hosts our business email.

These providers process your information only to provide their services to us, under their own privacy and security terms. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe: every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. One click and you're off the list.
  • Review, correct, or delete: email hello@staginggroundlabs.com and we'll update or delete the information we hold about you.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry details for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a business relationship, and update-list details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

Do Not Track

Because we don't track visitors across sites or over time, our site behaves the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal. We do not allow third parties to collect personal information about your online activities over time across websites through this site.

Children

This site is for working professionals and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above. Material changes will be noted at the top of the page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@staginggroundlabs.com.
