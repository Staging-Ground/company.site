07 // about

Founded on Engineering Experience

System Foundry was founded by Dwayne Pugh — a systems engineer and technical educator with more than a decade of experience building production systems across government, automotive technology, critical infrastructure, and enterprise platforms.

The company exists to help organizations build better engineering systems by developing both their technology and their people. We believe the strongest technical organizations are the ones that continuously grow their engineers' capabilities — not just their software.

Every engagement is guided by the same principle: help people understand why systems work — not simply how to operate them.
